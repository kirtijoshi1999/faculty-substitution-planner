Criteria

Eligible substitutes have at most the max lectures on the selected day (including adjustments already assigned) and are free in that period. They're ranked by that day's load first, then by weekly load.

No adjustments for (HOD, COE, AD …)

These faculty are never suggested or auto-assigned as substitutes. Their own lectures are still adjusted when they are absent.
